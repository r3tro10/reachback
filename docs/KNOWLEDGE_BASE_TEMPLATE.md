## How to Use This Template

## Best Practices

- **Keep answers concise:** SMS messages cap at 160 chars. Break longer answers across multiple Q&A entries.
- **Use natural language:** Write like you speak. Avoid corporate jargon.
- **Include calls-to-action:** End answers with "Call [PHONE]" or "Visit [WEBSITE]" when appropriate.
- **Avoid overpromising:** Be accurate about service areas, timelines, and pricing.
- **Update regularly:** Quarterly reviews to ensure accuracy and relevance.
